# False Positive Rules

These patterns look smart but usually produce weak work.

## Strategy False Positives

- A 2x2 matrix with no decision.
- A market map with no buyer or budget owner.
- "AI transformation" without workflow, data, governance, ROI, and adoption.
- TAM claims without a route to revenue.
- Competitor lists without segmentation axes.
- High-level recommendations with no owner or timeline.

## Research False Positives

- Funding news treated as demand proof.
- One viral social post treated as a market.
- Analyst category names treated as customer pain.
- Company marketing pages treated as adoption proof.
- Old sources used for current market claims.

## SME False Positives

- Growth plan before cash survival.
- Digital transformation before process standardization.
- New product plan before gross margin by product/customer is known.
- Succession plan without key-person risk and customer trust transfer.
- AI automation before the workflow is understood.

## Asset False Positives

- Pretty deck without a decision.
- Decorative icons.
- Dashboard with many charts and no action threshold.
- Article that summarizes but teaches no reusable idea.
- Skill that bundles everything but gives no routing logic.
