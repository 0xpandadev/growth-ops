# Succession And SME M&A

Use this for business succession, inherited companies, small-business acquisition, search funds, owner transition, and post-acquisition operating plans.

## Due Diligence Angles

- owner dependency
- customer concentration
- supplier concentration
- true margins
- receivables quality
- debt/tax/lease obligations
- key employee retention
- transferability of customer trust
- undocumented know-how
- local reputation
- price power
- modernization potential

## Transition Plan

First 30 days:

- preserve trust
- observe operations
- secure cash visibility
- document critical workflows
- meet key customers and employees

Days 31-90:

- standardize core processes
- create KPI dashboard
- identify margin leaks
- reduce owner bottlenecks
- test one growth or pricing lever

After 90 days:

- redesign offers
- modernize systems
- hire or outsource missing roles
- build repeatable sales/customer cadence

## Watchouts

- changing too much before trust transfer
- assuming old data is clean
- ignoring informal relationships
- buying revenue with no process
- overvaluing "AI modernization" before cash and workflows are stable
