## CFI-Style Valuation Method Selection

Pick the method based on the case:

- DCF: when cash-flow drivers and assumptions can be modeled.
- Comparable company analysis: when there are relevant public peers.
- Precedent transactions: when acquisition logic matters.
- Asset-based valuation: when assets, liquidation, or balance-sheet reality matters.
- Sum-of-the-parts: when business units have different economics.

Always include the key driver, sensitivity, and reason the chosen method fits.
