# Turnaround And Cash Control

Use this when a company is distressed, near insolvency, overleveraged, cash-constrained, shrinking, or operationally broken.

This is decision support, not legal, tax, insolvency, or financial advice. Suggest qualified professionals when needed.

## First Principle

Cash survival comes before growth.

## 13-Week Cash View

Track:

- opening cash
- expected receipts
- critical payments
- payroll
- tax/social insurance
- rent/lease
- loan payments
- supplier payments
- owner draws
- closing cash
- minimum required cash
- actions needed

## Turnaround Tree

1. Stop bleeding
2. Secure cash visibility
3. Protect core revenue
4. Renegotiate obligations where appropriate
5. Stop unprofitable work
6. Fix pricing and gross margin
7. Standardize critical operations
8. Rebuild sales and customer trust
9. Decide recovery, sale, wind-down, or pivot

## Japan SME Regeneration Checks

When the case is a Japanese SME, consider the public 中小企業庁 / 中小企業活性化協議会 logic:

- If effectively insolvent, can the plan clear effective insolvency within roughly 5 years?
- If ordinary profit is negative, can the plan turn ordinary profit positive within roughly 3 years?
- Can interest-bearing debt / cash flow fall to roughly 10x or less by the end of the plan?
- For very small businesses, can operating cash flow become positive for 3 consecutive fiscal years after the plan period starts?
- Is the business profitable at the operating level but financially constrained, or is the core business itself broken?

These are not legal requirements for the assistant to decide. Use them as practical diagnostic thresholds and recommend specialist support where needed.

## Transformation Office Discipline

Use transformation-office logic for execution:

- one source of truth
- weekly action-oriented cadence
- each initiative has owner, action, deadline, and value at stake
- finance validates the value logic
- blockers are escalated quickly
- progress is tracked through stages from idea to realized impact

## When To Combine With PE Value Creation

Load `pe-value-creation.md` when the user needs more than survival:

- acquisition or succession improvement plan
- post-takeover 100-day plan
- operational value creation
- revenue/margin expansion after stabilization
- initiative portfolio with owner, KPI, impact, confidence, and deadline

Do not use PE-style growth levers before cash survival, creditor reality, and must-pay obligations are understood.
