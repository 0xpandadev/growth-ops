# Legacy Company Modernization

Use this for old operating cultures, manual processes, low digital maturity, outdated sales, unclear data, and business pivot questions.

## Principle

Modernize the operating system before adding fashionable tools.

## Modernization Sequence

1. Map core workflows.
2. Identify bottlenecks and rework.
3. Standardize forms, statuses, and owners.
4. Create shared tables or systems of record.
5. Build basic KPI cadence.
6. Use AI to reduce repeated writing, search, routing, and analysis.
7. Redesign offers, pricing, and customer touchpoints.
8. Test new channels or pivots.

## Execution Rhythm

Borrow transformation-office discipline when modernization becomes more than a small fix:

- create a single initiative list
- each item has owner, value, deadline, and blocker
- hold a weekly action meeting, not a presentation meeting
- remove bottlenecks immediately
- keep finance/cash impact visible
- retire initiatives that do not move cash, customer experience, risk, or operating speed

## Lean / TPS Lens

Public reference:

- Toyota Production System: https://global.toyota/en/company/vision-and-philosophy/production-system/

Look for:

- waste
- waiting
- rework
- overprocessing
- handoff failure
- inventory mismatch
- unclear standards

## AI Modernization Lens

Good AI uses:

- summarize customer history
- extract fields from documents
- draft quotes/proposals
- detect anomalies
- create training materials
- turn tacit workflows into SOPs
- create management dashboards

Bad AI uses:

- automating a broken process
- adding chatbots with no knowledge base
- building dashboards with no decision owner
- replacing trust-heavy human work too early

## Output

Return:

- current operating bottlenecks
- standardization plan
- AI-ready data map
- 30/60/90-day modernization roadmap
- risks and change-management notes
