# Scoring Rubrics

Use scores to clarify tradeoffs, not to create false precision.

## Opportunity Score

Score 1-5:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Pain urgency | Is the pain frequent, costly, or time-sensitive? |
| Budget | Is there a buyer and money? |
| Timing | Why now? What trigger creates movement? |
| Reachability | Can the user reach early customers/accounts? |
| Differentiation | Is there a wedge or unfair angle? |
| Execution fit | Can the user execute with current assets? |
| Evidence strength | Is this supported by real signals? |

## Account Score

Score 1-5:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| ICP fit | Does this account match the target? |
| Pain fit | Is the likely pain visible? |
| Trigger | Is there a recent reason to act? |
| Budget proxy | Is there ability to pay? |
| Access | Can the user contact or be introduced? |
| Offer fit | Is there a clear proposal angle? |
| Timing risk | Is the timing wrong or slow? |

## SME Risk Score

Score 1-5, where high means riskier:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Cash pressure | How many weeks/months of runway? |
| Owner dependency | Does the business break without one person? |
| Customer concentration | Is revenue concentrated? |
| Margin opacity | Are true product/customer margins unknown? |
| Debt/tax pressure | Are fixed obligations dangerous? |
| Process fragility | Are key workflows undocumented? |
| Market decline | Is demand structurally shrinking? |

## Output Interpretation

Do not total scores blindly. Highlight:

- one or two decisive strengths
- one or two kill risks
- missing evidence
- best next test
