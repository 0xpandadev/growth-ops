## JTBD Lens

Customers do not only buy a product category. They hire a solution to make progress in a specific circumstance.

Extract:

- trigger: what happened right before the search or purchase?
- job: what progress is the customer trying to make?
- current workaround: what do they use now?
- push: what makes the current situation unacceptable?
- pull: what makes the new solution attractive?
- anxiety: what makes switching scary?
- habit: what keeps them from changing?
- outcome metric: how would the customer know the job is done?

## Bain Value Lens

Translate features into value elements:

- functional: saves time, reduces cost, quality, reduces effort, avoids hassle, organizes, connects
- economic: revenue growth, risk reduction, productivity, cash flow, pricing power
- emotional / trust: reduces anxiety, reputation, confidence, affiliation
- strategic: differentiation, loyalty, advocacy, resilience

Use this to avoid the commoditization trap. Do not compete only on price and performance if trust, risk, ease, status, or time-to-value matter more.

## PLG Fit Lens

Use PLG only when the product and market support it.

Check:

- marginal cost per user is low
- user can influence buyer or is the buyer
- product delivers visible value quickly
- onboarding can be self-serve or product-assisted
- product creates expansion, invite, collaboration, or usage loops
- sales can use product-qualified signals
- activation, retention, and expansion can be measured

If these are weak, recommend sales-led, service-led, content-led, or relationship-led growth instead.
